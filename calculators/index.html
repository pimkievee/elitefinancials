EXPLORE THE CALCULATORS

Choose a tool to get started.

Swipe left or right, tap a calculator, or use the arrow buttons.

01 / 05

Mortgage calculator

Estimate monthly mortgage payments and ongoing housing costs in Canada.

Estimated total per month—

Mortgage payment plus property tax and home insurance.

Uses Canadian semi-annual compounding for fixed-rate mortgage estimates. Excludes mortgage default insurance, utilities, closing costs, condo fees, rate changes and lender qualification.

02 / 05

Compound interest calculator

See how savings and regular contributions could grow over time.

Projected future value—

Illustrative balance after regular monthly contributions.

Assumes contributions at each month-end and a constant nominal annual rate compounded monthly. Returns are hypothetical, not guaranteed; taxes, fees and inflation are excluded.

03 / 05

Retirement calculator

Compare your projected savings with an estimated retirement income goal.

Projected retirement savings—

A planning estimate based on the inputs provided.

The target uses a simple first-year withdrawal-rate guideline, not a guaranteed lifetime income. Inflation is applied before retirement. Taxes, CPP, OAS, pensions, fees and post-retirement changes are excluded.

04 / 05

Debt payoff calculator

Estimate how long your credit card or loan balance could take to repay.

Estimated payoff time—

Based on consistent monthly payments, with no new charges.

Uses monthly interest at APR ÷ 12, rounded only for display. Actual credit card interest often accrues daily and payments may vary. No new borrowing or additional fees are assumed.

05 / 05

Life insurance calculator

Estimate a coverage starting point based on financial obligations and protection already in place.

Estimated coverage gap—

A needs-based starting estimate, not an insurance quotation.

Simple undiscounted income replacement plus obligations, less existing coverage and accessible savings. Does not determine eligibility, premiums, taxes, policy suitability or final insurable needs. Speak with a licensed professional.

01 / 05

These calculators are educational estimates only, not personal financial advice, insurance quotes or mortgage approvals. They do not guarantee investment performance or eligibility. Actual results may differ.
