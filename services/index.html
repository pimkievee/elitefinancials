Financial Planning Services

Practical planning support for the responsibilities you carry.

Elite Financials helps clients review protection, savings, retirement, education planning, business risks, and travel coverage with clear explanations and realistic next steps.

Financial planning service areas

01

Family Financial Planning

Organize priorities around income, expenses, debt, dependants, savings goals, and the decisions that affect your household.

02

Life Insurance Planning

Review coverage for income replacement, mortgage needs, family obligations, final expenses, and business responsibilities.

03

Retirement Planning

Clarify retirement goals, expected income needs, contribution habits, timelines, and the role of registered accounts.

04

Critical Illness & Disability Protection

Plan for the financial impact of illness or injury so income, debt payments, and family needs are not left unprotected.

05

RESP Education Planning

Help parents and guardians understand education savings options, grant opportunities, contribution habits, and long-term planning for children.

06

RRSP / TFSA / FHSA Guidance

Compare account options in plain language so your saving and investing choices match your goals and life stage.

07

Business Protection Planning

Support business owners with key person coverage, continuity needs, partner risk, and practical protection conversations.

08

Newcomer Financial Guidance

Help newcomers understand Canadian insurance, registered accounts, family planning, and financial systems step by step.

09

Travel Insurance

Coverage support for visitors to Canada, Super Visa, international students, families, and travellers outside Canada.

10

Financial Review & Checkup

Review what is already in place, identify gaps, and decide what should be updated, simplified, or prioritized.

11

Professional Opportunity Conversations

Explore financial services career and business opportunities for people interested in helping families understand protection and planning.
